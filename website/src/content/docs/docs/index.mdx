VigilOps is an **ops agent that lives on your server**. It runs as one Docker container next to your apps and handles the day-to-day work of keeping a server healthy:

- **Watches** your containers, disk, memory and health-check URLs every minute
- **Fixes** known problems on its own, like restarting a stopped container or clearing old Docker images when the disk fills up
- **Backs up** your Postgres, MySQL and MongoDB databases, on demand or on a schedule
- **Asks you** on Telegram before doing anything risky, and tells you what it did
- **Deploys** your apps with a health check and automatic rollback

You control it with the `vigil` command on the server, or from your phone through a Telegram bot.

The self-hosted version is free and complete. [VigilOps Cloud](/docs/guides/cloud/) (Pro and Team plans) adds managed backup storage, a dashboard for every backup, and alerts from outside your servers.

```bash
vigil status
vigil backup my-postgres
vigil backup schedule my-postgres daily 02:00
```

## How VigilOps is different

Tools like Terraform and Ansible **build and configure** infrastructure: you describe what a server should look like, and they make it so. VigilOps does the other half, **running** servers that already exist.

| | Terraform / Ansible | VigilOps |
|---|---|---|
| Job | Create and configure servers | Keep running servers healthy |
| When it runs | When you apply a change | All the time, every minute |
| You write | Configuration files and playbooks | Nothing. It inspects what's already running |
| Typical task | "Install Postgres on 10 servers" | "Postgres stopped at 3am, so restart it, then tell me" |

They work well together: provision with Terraform or Ansible, then let VigilOps look after the server.

## How it works

Every minute (configurable), VigilOps:

1. **Collects a snapshot:** container states, disk, memory, whether port 80 answers, and your health-check URLs.
2. **Checks built-in rules.** Known problems with a safe fix, like a stopped container or a full disk, are fixed right away.
3. **Escalates anything unusual to AI**, using whichever provider you configure. The AI proposes a fix, a suggestion that needs your approval, or an alert.
4. **Runs only allowlisted commands.** Anything outside a short list of safe commands is blocked, including AI suggestions.
5. **Logs every action** to an audit log and reports anything important on Telegram.

Backups, deploys and updates run whenever you ask for them, or on a schedule.

## Is VigilOps for me?

VigilOps is a good fit if you:

- run apps with Docker or Docker Compose on one or a few Linux servers (a VPS from Contabo, Hetzner, DigitalOcean, AWS and so on)
- don't have a dedicated ops team watching those servers
- want backups and basic self-healing without assembling a monitoring stack

It isn't meant to replace Kubernetes, a full observability platform like Prometheus + Grafana, or configuration management.
